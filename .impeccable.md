### Users

Adults (Brazilian, Portuguese-speaking) considering psychotherapy with Luiza. They arrive in private moments — often after weeks of hesitation, often on a phone, often anxious. They are not browsing for a service; they are deciding whether to trust a person with their inner life.

Three primary jobs they come to do:

1. **Decide whether to book a first session.** They want to know who Luiza is, how she works, whether her Jungian approach is what they need. The WhatsApp CTA / contact form is the conversion, but trust is built before they reach it.
2. **Read the blog for ideas and emotional support.** Returning visitors who treat the blog as ongoing companionship — essays on individuation, dreams, archetypes, shadow work. The blog is a primary surface, not SEO bait.
3. **Book or reschedule sessions** as existing clients (lower priority — admin task, not the design's center of gravity).

### Brand personality

Three words: **Warm · Human · Approachable** — interpreted through Jungian/depth-psychology craft, not through wellness-startup pastel cheer.

- **Warm** = earth pigments and parchment surfaces, not white-and-mint. The warmth of an old book read by lamplight, not a meditation app.
- **Human** = hand-textured, painterly, slightly imperfect. The opposite of vector-flat and algorithmically generated. Type with humanist character; ornament that looks painted.
- **Approachable** = contemplative, never clinical. Slow pace. Generous space. Plain language. The site invites rather than sells. No "Book Your Free Consultation Now" sticky banner.

Emotional goals: confidence, calm, curiosity. The visitor should feel they have arrived somewhere serious but not severe — a reading room, not a clinic.

### Aesthetic direction

**The page as an illuminated manuscript.** The references (`/references/01.jpg`, `02.jpg`, `03.jpg`, `05.jpg`, `06.jpg`, `logo.jpg`, plus `print01.png` — Instagram screenshot) sit firmly in the tradition of Carl Jung's *Red Book* (Liber Novus): hand-painted mandalas, alchemical illustration, archetypal symbols, mythic landscapes. The Instagram reference [@simbolos.do.self](https://www.instagram.com/simbolos.do.self/) confirms the same lineage. `logo.jpg` is the practice logo and may anchor brand identity (mandala with four cardinal-archetype circles).

Translated to a website:

- **Painterly, not generated.** Real painted assets (the client's actual artwork) carry the aesthetic. Avoid programmatically drawn mandalas, parametric SVG flourishes, AI-generated decoration.
- **Symbols are content, not decoration.** An archetypal image appears because it carries meaning at that point — section breaks, post openers, hero. Never as filler.
- **Earth pigments only.** Palette drawn from the references themselves: terracotta/rust, deep cobalt, ochre/saffron, forest moss, gilt accent, charcoal ink — on warm parchment cream. Never neon, never pastel-startup, never sterile grey-on-grey.
- **Editorial pacing.** Long-form essays. Generous margins. Single warm column of body text. Drop caps. Marginalia. Painted symbols at section breaks. Treat reading as contemplative.
- **Slow first impression.** Type-first hero. No stock photo above the fold. No urgency banner. Conversion through trust earned over the page.

**Theme**: Light, warm parchment. Not stark white — paper/vellum tones. Anxious users on phones at night; the site should not be foreboding, but it also should not be clinical.

**Typography direction** (commit at craft time, but starting points):
- **Display / Headings**: a literary serif with gravity — `Cardo` (Bembo-derived, scholarly, exquisite italic) is a strong starting point. Never `Fraunces`, `Cormorant`, `Playfair`, `Newsreader`, `Crimson` — those are the trained reflexes.
- **Body**: a warm humanist serif that holds at small sizes — `Vollkorn` ("whole grain" — robust, scholarly, slightly imperfect) or `EB Garamond` / `Source Serif 4` as alternates.
- **Italics matter.** Use them for emphasis the way printed books do. Cardo italic is part of the voice.
- All-serif is acceptable. A neutral sans for UI labels (`Manrope`, `Public Sans`) is acceptable but not required — small caps from the display face can carry nav and labels.
- All choices must support full Portuguese diacritics and Latin Extended.

**Color palette** (OKLCH, light theme, indicative — calibrate per context):
- Background (parchment): `oklch(0.97 0.012 75)` — warm cream, hint of ochre
- Surface (slightly raised): `oklch(0.94 0.018 75)`
- Foreground (ink): `oklch(0.22 0.02 35)` — warm near-black, never `#000`
- Muted text: `oklch(0.50 0.02 60)` — warm grey, tinted toward background
- Primary accent (terracotta — used sparingly): `oklch(0.55 0.16 35)`
- Secondary accent (cobalt — for the mandala-center moments): `oklch(0.40 0.14 250)`
- Tertiary accent (moss — rare, for tags or marginalia): `oklch(0.45 0.10 145)`
- Gilt highlight (used like gold leaf, not a gradient): `oklch(0.75 0.13 80)`
- Borders: tinted, low-contrast — `oklch(0.85 0.015 75)`

The 60-30-10 weight rule applies: 60% parchment + ink, 30% muted/secondary, 10% accent. Accents are *rare*; that is what makes them work.

### Anti-references (explicit)

These are what the site must NOT look like. If a layout drifts toward any of them, stop and rewrite.

- **Her current Google Sites page** ([sites.google.com/view/psicologa-luiza](https://sites.google.com/view/psicologa-luiza/p%C3%A1gina-inicial)) — the floor we are rising from. Templated Google Sites chrome, generic sans-serif, stock landscape photos as section headers, basic bulleted services list, "Skip to main content" boilerplate, Google Sites attribution and abuse-reporting links in the footer, no headshot, no distinctive type, no painterly identity. *Functional but understated.* Luiza herself has said it feels "too simple and amateur." The new site must read as **made by a designer** in a way the current one does not.
- **BetterHelp / Talkspace clinical telehealth aesthetic** — sky blue + white + cheerful sans-serif + sticky CTA. The opposite of this brief.
- **Wellness-startup pastel illustration** (Calm, Headspace, Mindbloom). Friendly mountains. Smiling abstract people. No.
- **Stock photography of therapy clichés** — hands holding hands, woman on cliff at sunset, journal with coffee, plant on windowsill. Use the client's actual painted references instead.
- **Generic "modern" tech site** — Inter sans-serif, dark mode with neon accents, gradient buttons, glassmorphism. Banned across the board.
- **Auto-generated geometric mandalas** — Spirograph SVG, parametric flourishes, "sacred geometry" generators. The references are *paintings*; honor that.
- **Greens-and-whites "natural therapy" palette** — sage + cream + rounded sans. Looks templated.
- **Cards-everywhere SaaS layout** — identical card grids with icon + heading + text. Editorial layout instead.
- **Border-left accent stripes, gradient text, side-stripe callouts** — banned per impeccable's absolute rules.

### Discoverability & agent-readability (SEO + AEO)

**Goal**: world-class SEO **and** AEO (Agentic Engine Optimization). The site must be (a) findable on Google for Brazilian Portuguese psychology / Jungian queries, and (b) consumable by AI coding agents and LLM-backed search (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini) when users ask about psychotherapy, Jungian therapy, or symbolic dreamwork in Portuguese. Both audiences are first-class.

**Implementation requirements** — these are not optional polish, they shape every page we build:

#### 1. Server rendering, semantic HTML, real URLs

- Next.js SSG/SSR for every public page. No client-only routes for content. Already enforced by the stack but verify per route.
- Strict heading hierarchy (`h1` → `h2` → `h3`, no skipping). One `h1` per page. Lead each section with the outcome / claim.
- Semantic landmarks: `<header>`, `<nav>`, `<main>`, `<article>`, `<aside>`, `<footer>`. No `<div>` soup.
- Real, slugged URLs in pt-BR (`/sobre`, `/blog/individuação-e-sombra`, `/contato`). No query-string routing for content.
- `lang="pt-BR"` on `<html>`. Diacritic-correct slugs allowed (Next handles Unicode URLs); decide once and stay consistent.

#### 2. Structured data (JSON-LD)

Embed in the document head, not via client-side injection. At minimum:

- **`Person`** for Luiza on every page (name, image, jobTitle, worksFor, sameAs, areaServed = Guarulhos/SP, knowsAbout = Jungian psychology, dreamwork, individuation).
- **`LocalBusiness` / `MedicalBusiness`** for the practice (NAP — name, address, phone — kept consistent across the site).
- **`Article` / `BlogPosting`** for every blog post (headline, datePublished, dateModified, author=Luiza, image, articleBody summary, keywords).
- **`FAQPage`** wherever there is a Q&A block (services pillars, "first session" page).
- **`Review` / `AggregateRating`** for the existing testimonials.
- **`BreadcrumbList`** for nested pages.

A `getJsonLd*` helper already exists at `src/ui/lib/jsonLd.tsx` — extend it; do not bypass it.

#### 3. Meta + social (already partially scaffolded via Payload settings)

- Title, description, canonical, robots, OG image, Twitter card on every route — derived from Payload `Settings` global with per-page override.
- One painted-asset OG image template (1200×630) that uses Luiza's actual references — distinct from the generic gradient OG everyone ships.
- `sitemap.ts` and `robots.ts` already exist (`src/app/(frontend)/`); audit them for: AI agents not blocked, all public routes listed, RSS feed referenced.

#### 4. Agent-readability surface

- **`/llms.txt`** at site root — flat Markdown index of all public content with one-line descriptions and (where useful) token counts. Generate from Payload at build time so it stays in sync.
- **`AGENTS.md`** in repo root — the current `CLAUDE.md` is already serving this role; either symlink `AGENTS.md → CLAUDE.md` or duplicate the design context there. Mention key API/sandbox links if any.
- **Markdown source accessible** for every blog post. Convention: appending `.md` to a post URL (`/blog/<slug>.md`) returns clean Markdown without nav/footer chrome. Implement as a Next route handler that pulls from Payload Lexical and serializes to Markdown.
- **"Copiar para IA" button** on blog posts — copies the clean Markdown (not rendered HTML) to the clipboard. Aesthetic must respect the manuscript brief — no generic icon button.
- **Token count metadata** — server-side estimate (chars/4) exposed as `<meta name="ai:token-count">` and included in `llms.txt`. Aim for <20K tokens per blog post; if longer, split.
- **Front-load the page.** First ~500 tokens of any page should answer: who is this, what does she do, how do you reach her. Agents have limited patience.
- Tables for parameter-like content (fees, modalities, hours) — they compress better than nested prose.

#### 5. Performance + Core Web Vitals

- LCP < 2.5s on 4G. Hero must not depend on hero-image fetch — type-first hero buys this for free.
- Painted reference assets are large; serve via `next/image` with proper sizes, AVIF/WebP, blur placeholder. Never ship a 2MB JPEG inline.
- Self-host fonts (`next/font/google`) with `display: swap` + subset for pt-BR. No FOIT, no third-party font CDN.
- No client-side analytics that block render. If GA is wired (`--with-ga` flag), load with `next/script` strategy="afterInteractive".

#### 6. Analytics segmentation (post-launch, not blocking design)

- Track AI referral sources (`chatgpt.com`, `claude.ai`, `perplexity.ai`, `gemini.google.com`, `copilot.microsoft.com`).
- Server-log AI-agent fingerprints (`axios/`, `curl/`, `got`, `colly`, `Mozilla` + Playwright markers). Distinguish from human traffic.
- Define one north-star metric: WhatsApp / contact-form completions. Everything else is secondary.

### Design principles

1. **The page is an illuminated manuscript.** Editorial pacing, generous margins, single warm column for body text, drop caps, marginalia, painted symbols at section breaks. Reading is contemplative, not transactional.
2. **Painterly, not generated.** Texture and irregularity are features. Real painted assets do the work; programmatic ornament does not. If something looks algorithmically clean, it is wrong here.
3. **Symbols carry meaning.** Archetypal imagery (mandalas, alchemical figures) appears only where it earns its place. Never as decoration, never as flourish.
4. **Earth pigments, used rarely.** Terracotta, cobalt, ochre, moss, gilt — drawn from the client's references. Accents are sparing; their power is in their rarity. No neon, no pastel, no gradient.
5. **Trust, not urgency.** No sticky CTAs, no countdown timers, no "limited spots." The visitor decides to book because the page gave them a clear, calm sense of the practitioner. Conversion is earned, not pressured.
6. **Designed for two audiences at once.** Every page must be scannable and beautiful for humans **and** structured, semantic, token-efficient for AI agents. The two disciplines reinforce each other; if a design choice satisfies one but breaks the other, it is the wrong choice.

### Carve-outs

Site-wide rules above stand by default. Specific, named, scoped suspensions live here. Any new suspension requires a new entry; do not generalize an existing one.

#### Cosmos (celestial atlas section, home page)

**Scope.** `src/ui/home/Cosmos*` and the home page composition that consumes it (`src/app/(frontend)/page.tsx`). Nowhere else.

**Suspended.** Principle 2 (painterly, not generated) and Principle 3 (symbols never as decoration) are suspended *only* inside the scope above.

**What is permitted inside the scope.**
- Parametric star positions, twinkle, and constellation line stroke animation, rendered via Three.js / `@react-three/fiber`.
- Cursor-driven parallax up to ±5° on the constellation layer (deprecated in v2 of the section, but still allowed if reintroduced).
- The twelve zodiac sigils rendered as small painted-style cartouches (texture-mapped planes) or, as a stub during build, as Unicode glyphs in Cardo italic.
- A scroll-pinned cinema timeline of ~200–250vh mapped to the section's enter/approach/orbit/tilt/recede phases.
- An armillary sphere built from real 3D geometry: 5 concentric torus rings + a central gilt sun (sphere or disc), rendered with painted texture maps + `MeshBasicMaterial` (no real lighting, highlights baked into textures). Autonomous slow rotation + secondary wobble are permitted.
- A multi-layer parallax star field (3 Z-layers of painted ink-dot sprites) and a scroll-driven perspective camera path.
- Sparse, painted cosmic atmosphere: 3–5 textured nebula cloud planes at deep Z (semi-transparent, drifting); a single textured Milky Way band plane at deep Z; one painted comet at a time on an autonomous spawn/despawn timer with hand-tuned curving trajectories. All in the same earth-pigment palette as the rest of the site (terracotta, cobalt, ochre, moss, gilt) — no neon, no bloom, no real lighting.

**What is still forbidden inside the scope.**
- Parametric mandalas, generated "sacred geometry," AI-flourish ornament.
- Dark / starfield-simulation ground. The section stays on parchment.
- AI-generated imagery for any texture asset. Texture work uses public-domain Renaissance source material (Bayer, Cellarius) recolored to the site palette, or commissioned hand-painted assets — never AI image generation.
- Real lighting, glow filters, atmospheric blur, or post-processing bloom. Light is *painted into* textures, not computed.
- Particles, instanced symbols, or generated ornament beyond the explicit elements named above (stars, constellation lines, armillary rings, sun, sigil cartouches, nebulae, MW band, comets).
- Any astrological reading or predictive language. The section frames the zodiac as Jungian *vocabulary*, not prediction. A short disclaimer line beneath the section makes this professionally legible.

**Why this carve-out exists.** The home page's emotional contract is well served by a moment of wonder right after the type-first hero — the practice's stated work (individuation, depth, archetypes) lives in the territory the section evokes. The risk (that this becomes the *real* identity of the site) is managed by keeping the section parchment-toned, by framing it as "vocabulary not prediction," and by limiting its scroll real estate so the column-based reading rhythm remains the dominant page rhythm.

**Adding to the carve-out.** Any parametric ornament outside the scope above — even within `src/ui/home/Cosmos*` if it goes beyond the permitted list — requires a new carve-out entry here. Do not extend this one silently.
