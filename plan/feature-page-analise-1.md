# Introduction

![Status: Completed](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-Completed-brightgreen)

This plan executes **TASK-037** of [`plan/architecture-site-restructure-1.md`](./architecture-site-restructure-1.md) under its per-page ritual (PAT-001): the approach page, built from the `page-analise` global that Phase 4 created and the primitives Phase 5 built, following the precedents in [`plan/feature-page-inicio-1.md`](./feature-page-inicio-1.md) and [`plan/feature-page-primeira-conversa-1.md`](./feature-page-primeira-conversa-1.md).

The page's job, in CONCEPT's words: **understand what Jungian analysis with her is and how it works, enough to begin.** And CONCEPT §6 states the thing that makes this page different from the other seven — **for análise, the approach _is_ the product.** There is no deliverable to describe, no fixed number of meetings, no test at the end. What a visitor is buying is a way of listening, so the page has to make that way of listening legible without promising anything it cannot promise.

It is also the site's strongest non-brand search asset after `/orientacao-profissional`: CONCEPT §10 assigns `/analise` the "análise junguiana / terapia junguiana online" cluster. So this is the deepest read on the site _and_ the one whose first screen has to answer a cold searcher in one paragraph.

Two policies bind harder here than anywhere else, and both are load-bearing rather than decorative:

- **REQ-007 — the wheel is visual-only.** `src/view/mandala/Symbols.tsx` arrived from the retired `/simbolos` route rendering `ZODIAC_CONTENT[sign].paragraph` and `VEDIC_CONTENT[sign].paragraph` — twenty-four dev-authored paragraphs and thirty-six dev-authored `motif` lines, every one of them flagged `_isPlaceholder: true`. Mounting that on `/analise` would have shipped prose in her name on the page she is most likely to be judged by.
- **CONCEPT §11 — no promised outcomes.** Individuação is the most attractive single word analytical psychology has to offer a stranger, and "tornar-se quem você realmente é" is one comma away from a guarantee. The temptation is structural, not accidental.

## 0. Shape brief

Written from the documents rather than from an interactive shape run (Phase 6 brief §0). The direction contract was already settled — CONCEPT §6 fixes the sections and their order, the global's eight tabs already match it, DESIGN fixes every visual call — so only what was materially open needed resolving. Mode: **Persuade**, but a slow persuasion: this page converts by being the most convincing thing on the site to read, not by asking.

- **Sequence: what it is → what it sees → how it works → the vocabulary those tools speak → what people actually arrive with → the method performed once → the facts → the ask.** That is the global's tab order and it is already an argument. The wheel lands at position four for a reason: by then the page has said that the analysis reads dreams, images and repetitions, and the wheel is what those readings are made of — arriving earlier it would be an astrology widget, arriving later it would be an afterthought.
- **Focal moment: the painted wheel**, and only the wheel (DESIGN: one wow per page). It is a real painted canvas, and the interaction is a magnifying glass over her own image — hover or select a sector, the rest of the painting desaturates, the chosen figure turns up to twelve o'clock. Nothing is generated; nothing is predicted.
- **Sonho ampliado is deliberately quiet.** It is greenlit (REQ-008) and it is the only idea on the creative menu that _demonstrates_ the method rather than decorating the page — but it sits two sections after the wheel, and DESIGN allows one set-piece. So it ships as a **still composition**: the dream on the left, its parallels gathered on the right. No pinning, no reveal, no interaction at all.
- **One plate, at the idea it amplifies.** PAT-002 wants a plate; it goes in **a visão**, beside individuação. Amplificação is her own method (CONCEPT §5) — setting an image beside an idea — so the plate performs the page's own subject on the page's own argument. The wheel is a painted asset too, but the two roles stay distinct: the plate amplifies, the wheel is the set-piece.
- **Tonal rhythm: exactly two events.** `parchment-deep` under the **mandala**, so the wheel reads as a set-piece rather than as a second plate, and under **para começar**, where `Comecar` already puts it. Everything between is plain parchment. Sonho ampliado is deliberately _not_ deep — a second tonal event there would make it compete with the wheel for the page's one moment of wonder.
- **Anti-goals.** No zodiac language that predicts anything, ever — the intro says the signs are vocabulary in her own words rather than leaving it to a reviewer to notice. No tracked-caps eyebrow per section (the global's `oQueTrazem.eyebrow` field is removed rather than left unrendered). No card grid for the three tools or the three pillars. No numerals on the tools — they happen at once. No second CTA style: one terracotta block, in the last section. No `FAQPage` or `Service` JSON-LD from this page. No portrait: the person is `/sobre`'s subject.

## 1. Requirements & Constraints

- **REQ-001**: All seven CONCEPT §6 sections ship in the map's order — abertura · a visão · o método · a mandala · o que as pessoas trazem · sonho ampliado · prático · para começar — preceded by the credencial strip under the opening.
- **REQ-002**: Every visitor-facing string reads from `page-analise` or from A Clínica, never hardcoded (master plan GUD-002). Only scaffolding a visitor cannot meaningfully edit lives in `messages/{pt,en}.json`: the wheel's accessible description and tablist name, the resting hint, the five reference-field labels, the two Vedic headings, and the two placeholder captions.
- **REQ-003**: Both locales render; untranslated fields fall back to Portuguese through Payload's `fallback: true` (master plan RISK-001).
- **REQ-004**: **The wheel is visual-only** (master plan REQ-007 / CONCEPT §11, §14.2). Prose about a sign renders only from her `reading` / `vedicReading` fields; the interlocking scholarly reference — element · modality · ruler · body · archetype, and each lunar mansion's deity · ruler · symbol — may render, because it is nomenclature rather than editorial voice (the Phase 4 notes say so explicitly).
- **REQ-005**: **Individuação is described as a concept, never as the reader's guaranteed result** (CONCEPT §11). No section, tool, plate caption or fact row may imply an outcome.
- **REQ-006**: **Symbols are vocabulary, never prediction** (CONCEPT §11, DESIGN §6). No astrological or predictive language anywhere on the page, and nothing on it reads the visitor (SEC-001): no birthdate, no "your sign", no per-visitor branching, no storage.
- **REQ-007**: AEO front-load (master plan REQ-012): the abertura carries what the analysis is · for whom · frequency · languages · reach, as semantic HTML with exactly one `h1`, and the `h1` carries the search term the page targets.
- **REQ-008**: **Sonho ampliado** ships as structure (master plan REQ-008 / CONCEPT §9.3) and never becomes a second wow: a static composition, no scroll-pinning, no elaborate reveal.
- **REQ-009**: `PraticoSection` with `fees="analysis"` — this page quotes analysis alone (a comparing reader is not choosing between the two doors here). `Comecar` with `opener="analysis"`, so the message that reaches her WhatsApp says which door it came through (CONCEPT §8.1).
- **REQ-010**: The section ends on the boundary line routing "qual profissão" to `/orientacao-profissional` (CONCEPT §4). The overlap at pillar III is the bridge, not a bug.
- **REQ-011**: **Her verbatim copy is preserved byte for byte.** `oQueTrazem`'s five-paragraph intro and the three pillars are hers; they existed only in the database until TASK-026 rescued them into `seed/pages.ts`, and this build moves them into `ANALISE_DEFAULTS` without changing a character.
- **CON-001**: Online-only. No section, tool, fact row, plate caption or placeholder label may imply a room a patient walks into (master plan CON-001).
- **CON-002**: Layered architecture (master plan CON-003): `infrastructure/payload/` accessor → `domain/analise/` type + mapper + action → route fetches and passes props → `view/analise/` renders. No component sees a raw Payload shape; nothing in `src/domain/` imports React, Next or next-intl.
- **CON-003**: The wheel is the only client component on the page, and **its still form is in the server-rendered HTML** (Phase 6 brief rule 2) — the painting, the twelve sectors and the resting hint, not an empty box.
- **CON-004**: WCAG 2.1 AA. The wheel is keyboard-operable with a visible focus ring, and every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative (master plan TASK-049 will audit it).
- **CON-005**: `pnpm build` peaks near 3.5 GB RSS and stays pressure-sensitive (master plan CON-004). Not run in this pass — the integrator owns it.
- **GUD-001**: DESIGN.md governs every visual call — all-serif, the plates the only saturation, terracotta the one recurring accent, flat on parchment, near-sharp edges, one drop cap for the page, the two-voices rule on every text element, `.roman-numeral` only where a sequence genuinely is ordered.
- **GUD-002**: Operational facts (fee, frequency, duration, time zone) are body type or the credential strip, never decorative small type (DESIGN's Marginalia-Is-Voice rule).
- **PAT-001**: Placeholder policy (master plan REQ-005): every empty asset slot renders a labeled `MediaPlaceholder`; never a stock stand-in, never a vector stand-in for a painted asset.
- **PAT-002**: Compose the Phase 5 primitives and the promoted page grammar rather than re-implementing them: `PageSection`, `SectionHeading`, `SectionLink`, `Credencial`, `Plate`, `MediaPlaceholder`, `RichTextProse`, `PraticoSection`, `Comecar`, `useMotionAllowed`.
- **PAT-003**: One concept per file, named exports, no barrels.

**Execution notes (2026-08-05)**

- **As built.** `src/domain/analise/{Analise,analiseFromPayload,analiseFromPayload.test,getAnalise}.ts` · `src/infrastructure/payload/getPageAnaliseGlobal.ts` · `src/view/analise/{Abertura,AVisao,OMetodo,Mandala,MandalaWheel,OQueTrazem,SonhoAmpliado}.tsx` · `src/app/(frontend)/[locale]/(pages)/analise/page.tsx` · `src/domain/zodiac/zodiacContent.test.ts` · rewritten `src/domain/zodiac/zodiacContent.ts` · amended `src/payload/globals/pages/analise.ts`. Deleted: `src/view/mandala/Symbols.tsx`, `src/domain/zodiac/MandalaContent.ts`. No `messages/*.json`, `seed/**`, `site/**`, `globals.css` or `view/general/**` edits — those are in the handoff.

- **Eight decisions the task text left open.**
  1. **The dev-authored prose was deleted, not merely unrendered.** The twenty-four paragraphs and thirty-six motifs are gone from `zodiacContent.ts`, and a test asserts the module carries no `paragraph`, `motif` or `_isPlaceholder` key at all. Leaving them in place, unread, would have kept a loaded gun in the repository: the next person to touch the wheel finds twelve ready-made paragraphs and one obvious place to wire them, and REQ-007 fails silently on the page she is judged by. Git history holds the text if anyone ever wants it as a starting point, which is the right place for a draft nobody may publish.
  2. **The nakshatra `motif` counts as authored prose, not as reference.** This was the specific open point. "A chama que separa e revela" is a poetic gloss on the symbol `lâmina`, written by a developer inside an object flagged `_isPlaceholder: true` — it fails the authorship test in exactly the way the sign paragraphs do. The scholarly datum is already carried by `symbol`; the motif was an interpretation of it. Deleted. The mansion still renders its name, its pada range, its deity, its ruler and its symbol, which is a complete reference entry.
  3. **`id="mandala"`, and the old `#simbolos` fragment is allowed to fall through.** `/simbolos` still 308s to `/analise`, and a browser reapplies the fragment to the redirect target — so `/simbolos#simbolos` would have landed on the wheel had the id been kept. It was not: the section is "A mandala" in CONCEPT §6, in the global's tab, and in its own heading, and an anchor named after a deleted address is a name every future reader has to decode. A stale fragment now lands at the top of `/analise`, which is where a visitor arriving from a retired route should land — the page's opening is the answer to whatever brought them. The deep link is also implausible: the old `#simbolos` anchored the retired page to itself.
  4. **Sonho ampliado renders, gated on the motif, and the motif ships drafted.** The question was structure-with-placeholders versus hide-until-written, and the honest answer is neither extreme. The **motif** is quoted speech — the words a dreamer would use, taken from CONCEPT §9.3's own example — so drafting it puts no prose in her name, exactly the reasoning that let the four bilhete openers ship as drafts. The **intro** states what amplificação is, a fact about her craft CONCEPT §5 already fixes. The **parallels** are her curation ("her words required"), so each renders only once it has a line, a painting, or at least a named work; three labeled empty frames beside a dream would be scaffolding a visitor has to look at, which is the failure mode the primeira-conversa build already rejected once. At launch the section is therefore a complete small composition — what amplificação is, and one motif — that grows into §9.3 as she curates. Clearing the motif removes it entirely.
  5. **The motif is the one field with no code fallback, and that is deliberate.** Every other string on the page falls back when blank; this one does not, because a fallback would make "clear the field to remove the section" unreachable without a deploy. The consequence is that `analiseFromPayload({})` and `ANALISE_DEFAULTS` disagree on exactly one value — which the mapper's comment states and its first test asserts, so the inconsistency cannot be "fixed" by accident. `ANALISE_DEFAULTS` still carries a real motif, so a page rendered with Payload off shows the section rather than losing it.
  6. **`sonhoAmpliado.parallels` is the only array on the page that reads `[]` literally.** The Phase 6 brief asks for a per-array decision. `oMetodo.tools`, `oQueTrazem.pillars` and `pratico.items` all belong to sections CONCEPT §6 requires by name and none has a designed empty state, so an empty array is read as un-edited and falls back — the pillars most of all, since they are her own words and an untouched tab must never delete them. `parallels` has a designed partial state _by construction_: a parallel appears when she curates it, so all three being absent is the launch state rather than a fault, and falling back would print three labels with nothing to read.
  7. **`oQueTrazem.eyebrow` was removed from the global rather than left unrendered.** DESIGN §6 names a tracked-caps kicker above a section as scaffolding rather than voice, and no section on this site renders one — so the field could never reach the page. A field that cannot render is a field she types into a void. Nothing ever wrote it (Phase 4 seeded only heading, intro, note and pillars), so the drop loses nothing. Same class of decision as Início dropping the duplicated `credencial` tab.
  8. **The wheel's image stays a code asset.** `wheelGeometry` is calibrated to these exact pixels — `WHEEL_START_ANGLE_DEG = 33`, the annulus between radii 142 and 325 of a 690 viewBox — so a different painting uploaded into a `mediaSlot` would silently misalign all twelve sectors with no error anywhere. The asset is versioned with the code that measures it, and the comment says so. The cost is recorded in RISK-002.

- **Three defects in the inherited component, found and fixed.**
  1. **Two live regions announced every sign change.** `Symbols.tsx` had an `<output aria-live="polite">` under the wheel _and_ `aria-live="polite" aria-atomic="true"` on the detail panel — so hovering or arrowing through the wheel announced the sign's name and then re-read the entire five-row `<dl>`, the three mansions and both paragraphs, on every move. There is now one association and no live region: the sectors are `role="tab"` with `aria-controls` pointing at a `role="tabpanel"`, the focused sector's own label announces the sign, and the visible line under the wheel is `aria-hidden` because it duplicates that label exactly.
  2. **Twelve tab stops in the middle of a reading page.** Every sector was `tabIndex={0}`, so a keyboard visitor crossing the page pressed Tab twelve times through a decorative wheel to reach the next section. It is now a `role="tablist"` with a roving `tabIndex` — one tab stop, arrow keys moving between neighbouring signs with automatic activation, `Home`/`End` jumping to Áries and Peixes, and circular wrap-around, which is what a wheel should do. Selection is set before focus moves, so the panel never blinks back to its resting state between two sectors.
  3. **A passing mouse could close a panel the keyboard was reading.** `onBlur` cleared the active sign, and `onMouseLeave` cleared it regardless of where focus was. Now blur never clears, and mouse-leave checks that focus is not still inside the wheel — so a keyboard visitor can arrow to a sign and then Tab forward into the panel to read it, which is the whole point of the `tabpanel` association.

- **Two findings worth recording.**
  1. **Reduced motion needed more than a disabled transition.** The inherited component had `motion-reduce:transition-none` on the rotating wrapper, which does not remove the rotation — it removes the _animation of_ the rotation, so a reduced-motion visitor got an instantaneous 90° snap of a large image, which is a worse experience than the smooth version rather than a gentler one. The transform is now gated on `useMotionAllowed()`: under reduced motion the wheel does not turn at all, and the desaturation plus the highlighted sector still mark the chosen figure. The hook already starts closed, so the server-rendered HTML is the still wheel either way.
  2. **A surviving mutant in the parallels filter.** Dropping the `plate.image !== null` term from `parallelsFrom` passed the whole suite: the only image-bearing parallel in the tests also carried a `painter`, so the provenance term kept it alive and the image term was never load-bearing. That is the likelier real case inverted — she uploads the detail she found and types the credit afterwards. Added the missing case; all four terms of the filter now kill a mutant.

- **Copy that needs her sign-off.** Everything except `oQueTrazem`'s intro and the three pillars, which are hers verbatim and must never be reworded. The drafts state only facts CONCEPT already fixes: the abertura (§6's "espaço seguro de escuta, reflexão e transformação" and the symptom as a call), a visão (§6's whole person and individuação, with §11's no-promise clause written into the prose), o método (§6's free non-judgemental dialogue and the three symbolic tools, closing on her own "é um trabalho de colaboração"), the mandala intro (§11's symbols-are-vocabulary rule), the boundary line (§4's routing sentence), Sonho ampliado's intro and motif (§5's amplificação, §9.3's example dream), prático (§6's weekly · on-line · pt/en · Brasil e exterior, §8.9's Brasília anchor) and para começar. Three need her attention specifically because they are **policy rather than description**: prático's "A análise não tem prazo fixo … quem decide seguir ou parar é você", prático's fifty-minute session length (carried across from the primeira-conversa drafts, never confirmed), and `oQueTrazem`'s heading and frentes note, which came from TASK-026's seed as unattributed literals and are treated here as drafts. TASK-052 owns the review.

- **Deliberately not done.**
  - **The plate is a labeled frame.** PAT-002 is satisfied structurally; the painting arrives through the CMS with no deploy, and a vector stand-in would invert the idea into the generated ornament DESIGN bans.
  - **The wheel's scholarly nomenclature is Portuguese in both locales.** The chrome around it localizes — the accessible description, the tablist name, the hints, the five field labels, the two Vedic headings — but the values those labels name come from `zodiacContent.ts`, which is code data with no locale mechanism. So `/en` reads `Element — fogo`, `Ruler — Marte`. Translating it is ~68 short strings of closed-set vocabulary and is purely additive (either a `messages` lookup keyed by value, or `ZODIAC_CONTENT_EN` beside the pt record); doing it inside a page build would have meant inventing an English scholarly vocabulary with no review, which is the sprawl CONCEPT §11 guards against. Recorded in the handoff as a named gap for TASK-044 or her English polish pass, not hidden.
  - **No hidden all-twelve mirror for crawlers.** `Symbols.tsx` carried a `<div hidden>` duplicating every sign's data. Dropped: crawlers discount hidden content, it would now also duplicate her readings once written, and — the real reason — twelve blocks of astrological reference data on the page that targets "análise junguiana online" is a topical liability. `/analise` must read as depth psychology, not as astrology. Accessibility does not pay for it: the tablist plus tabpanel reaches every sign by keyboard.
  - **No mobile substitute for the wheel**, because it does not need one. Unlike the Cosmos it is not desktop-only: an annulus sector is roughly 93 × 62 px at 86vw, which clears the 44 px target, and the layout stacks to wheel-above-panel. DESIGN's rule is about a set-piece that would otherwise be a hidden hole; this one is present at every width.
  - **No `FaqJsonLd` and no `Service` payload from this route.** The two services are declared once by the shared `(pages)` layout's entity graph, and `FAQPage` stays on `/perguntas` (TASK-032, TASK-040).
  - **No Jung passage.** CONCEPT §6 does not ask for one on this page, `JungPassage` renders nothing while her pool is empty, and the section that would carry one — Sonho ampliado's third parallel — is precisely where her curated passage belongs once it exists.

## 3. Alternatives

- **ALT-001**: Keep the twenty-four sign paragraphs and thirty-six motifs in `zodiacContent.ts`, unrendered. Rejected: REQ-007 becomes a convention a component has to remember rather than a property of the data, and the next person to touch the wheel finds ready-made prose and an obvious place to wire it. Deleting made the invariant structural and let a test enforce it.
- **ALT-002**: Render the nakshatra `motif` lines as reference data. Rejected: they are poetic glosses on the `symbol` field, written by a developer inside objects flagged `_isPlaceholder`. The scholarly datum is the symbol; the motif is an interpretation of it, which is the authorship line CONCEPT §11 draws.
- **ALT-003**: Ship Sonho ampliado with three labeled empty frames until her parallels exist ("structure with placeholders", read literally). Rejected: three unfilled boxes beside a dream is scaffolding a visitor has to look at, and REQ-005's `MediaPlaceholder` idiom is for a missing _asset_, not for a missing sentence. The launch state chosen instead — what amplificação is, plus one motif — is a complete composition that grows rather than a frame that waits.
- **ALT-004**: Hide Sonho ampliado entirely until she writes a parallel. Rejected: the section would be invisible on the very preview she reviews, which is the argument that got the bilhete openers drafted, and the motif is quoted speech rather than prose in her name — there is nothing to withhold.
- **ALT-005**: Give the motif a code fallback like every other string, for consistency. Rejected: it would make the section's off-switch unreachable without a deploy. The one-field inconsistency is documented in the mapper and asserted by a test, which is cheaper than a CMS field that cannot be cleared.
- **ALT-006**: Keep `id="simbolos"` on the wheel so `/simbolos#simbolos` still lands on it. Rejected: the section is "A mandala" everywhere else, an id named after a deleted route is a name every future reader must decode, the old anchor pointed the retired page at itself and is therefore implausible as an external deep link, and a stale fragment now lands at the top of `/analise` — the page's opening, which is the right answer for someone arriving from an address that no longer exists. Also rejected: carrying both ids, since two names for one section is exactly what rots.
- **ALT-007**: Make the wheel's painting a `mediaSlot` in the `mandala` tab so she can replace it. Rejected: `wheelGeometry`'s calibration is bound to these pixels, so a replacement uploaded through a field would misalign twelve sectors silently. Replacing the wheel is a code change by construction, and the comment says so.
- **ALT-008**: Keep the twelve sectors as twelve `role="button"` tab stops (the inherited implementation). Rejected: twelve Tab presses to cross a decorative wheel is a keyboard trap in everything but name, and a wheel is the one shape where arrow-key navigation is obviously right.
- **ALT-009**: Keep the `<div hidden>` mirror of all twelve signs for crawlers. Rejected: hidden content is discounted, it would duplicate her readings once written, and twelve blocks of zodiac reference data would drift `/analise`'s topical signal from depth psychology toward astrology — against the one search cluster CONCEPT §10 assigns this page. Also rejected: rendering the twelve as a visible list, which would create a second focal block competing with the wheel.
- **ALT-010**: Translate the wheel's nomenclature into English in this pass. Rejected here as scope: ~68 strings of scholarly vocabulary invented inside a page build, unreviewed, with no browser to check the result. It is additive, it changes no schema, and it is flagged with two concrete routes in the handoff.
- **ALT-011**: Put the plate in the mandala section, beside the wheel. Rejected: two paintings in one section, one of them the page's set-piece, and neither reads as what it is. The plate belongs where an image amplifies an idea — individuação — which is amplificação performed on the page's own argument.
- **ALT-012**: Quote both fees on this page so a reader can compare the two doors. Rejected: `fees="analysis"` is the existing rule (`useFeeRows`), and a reader on `/analise` is not choosing between the doors — the boundary line and the link to `/orientacao-profissional` are how the comparison is offered.

## 7. Risks & Assumptions

- **RISK-001**: **The wheel's ARIA tablist could not be heard.** `role="tab"` on SVG `<path>` elements with a roving `tabIndex` is correct on paper and focusable in every current browser, but no screen reader was available in this pass. If the `aria-controls` → `tabpanel` association does not carry a reader from the wheel into the panel, the fallback is to make the panel's heading a focusable landmark or to restore a single concise live region — not to restore the two that were there before. TASK-049 owns the audit.
- **RISK-002**: **`/art/wheel.jpg` has unverified provenance, and replacing it means recalibrating the geometry.** CONCEPT §7 requires verified public-domain status for every image and warns that several current picks fail; the footer colophon already claims verified provenance, so shipping an unverified wheel makes that line false. And because `wheelGeometry` is measured off these pixels, a replacement is a code change, not an upload. Flagged as a launch gate in the handoff.
- **RISK-003**: **The seed re-write can overwrite her admin edits.** `seed.snippet.ts` writes eight sections into `page-analise`, where TASK-026 had written only `oQueTrazem`. If she has edited the production global since, seeding would replace those edits. Mitigation: the handoff says to read the prod global before seeding it, and every value written comes from `ANALISE_DEFAULTS`, so nothing is lost that is not recoverable from code.
- **RISK-004**: **The `/en` wheel reads half in Portuguese.** Chrome localizes, the nomenclature does not. It is a recorded gap with two concrete additive fixes rather than a defect to discover later, but it is the one part of this page that a reviewer of the English mirror will notice first.
- **RISK-005**: A future refactor "fixing" the motif's missing fallback for consistency would silently kill the section's off-switch. Mitigation: the mapper comment and the test `lets a cleared motif hide the section`.
- **RISK-006**: The wheel's JPEG is served unoptimized (302 KB, no AVIF/WebP) because it is referenced from inside an SVG `<image href>`, where `next/image` cannot go. It is below the fold and inside the LCP budget, but it will show up in a Lighthouse pass; the route out is a hand-built `<picture>` or a pre-generated AVIF variant, not `next/image`.
- **ASSUMPTION-001**: `page-analise` in production holds only what TASK-026 seeded, so adding four fields and dropping `oQueTrazem.eyebrow` loses nothing. The `eyebrow` drop is safe either way — nothing ever wrote it.
- **ASSUMPTION-002**: English copy falls back to Portuguese wherever she has not translated (master plan RISK-001) — accepted, not a defect of this page.
- **ASSUMPTION-003**: The drafted copy is stated facts from CONCEPT, not her voice, and is labeled as such in `ANALISE_DEFAULTS` and listed above. `oQueTrazem`'s intro and its three pillars are the exception and are hers verbatim.
- **ASSUMPTION-004**: `ZODIAC_SIGN_IDS` and `WHEEL_ZODIAC` agree, which is what makes the one `as ZodiacSignId` narrowing in `MandalaWheel.tsx` safe. `zodiacContent.test.ts` asserts it, and both a dropped sign and a reordered pair kill a test.
