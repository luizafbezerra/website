# Introduction

![Status: Completed](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-Completed-brightgreen)

This plan executes **TASK-035** of [`plan/architecture-site-restructure-1.md`](./architecture-site-restructure-1.md) under its per-page ritual (PAT-001): the home page, rebuilt from the pre-CONCEPT single-page site into the eleven sections CONCEPT §6 specifies, reading the `page-inicio` global that Phase 4 created and composing the primitives Phase 5 built.

The page's job, in CONCEPT's words: **decide in one scroll that she is my person — and which door is mine.** Everything here serves that one sentence. The scroll opens type-first (the lockup, her positioning sentence, her lead), earns trust down the page, and ends at a WhatsApp conversation that was never asked for under pressure.

The home currently renders from `HOME_DEFAULTS` in code — a deliberate freeze taken in Phase 4 (TASK-025's owner decision) so a schema diff would not smuggle in a page rewrite. This plan thaws it: the CMS becomes the source, the old five-component `src/view/home/` retires, and the configurable section-order model (`src/domain/sections/`) goes with it, because CONCEPT's pages are fixed compositions (master plan ALT-002).

**Two decisions were taken before writing this plan** and are treated as settled inputs:

1. **The Instagram bridge is a horizontal tile row that un-crops in place** (REQ-010 of the master plan reserved this for the Início shape run; chosen by the owner 2026-08-04). Four to six square crops in the feed's own rhythm, drag/swipe, no arrows; tapping one expands it so the rest of the canvas fades in around the familiar square, with painter · title · year and the Jung passage she paired. Rejected: a static 2×3 grid (six squares in a grid sits against DESIGN's "don't build cards-everywhere grids"), and a single featured plate with a quiet tile row (quietest, but the un-crop reveal — a greenlit touch, REQ-008 — becomes secondary).
2. **The credential strip is A Clínica's, not the page's.** `page-inicio.credencial.items` duplicates `clinica.identity.credentials`, which Phase 5 created as the cross-page home for exactly this strip (CONCEPT §8.8: one strip on every core page). The duplicate tab is removed rather than rendered.

## 0. Shape brief

The `impeccable` shape run (PAT-001) found the direction contract already settled — PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md define the world, CONCEPT §6 defines the composition — so it resolved only what was materially open: the opening's first two screens.

- **Job and audience.** A follower arrives from the Instagram bio link asking "is this a real practice?"; a cold pt-BR searcher arrives anxious, on a phone, at night, comparing therapists. Mode: **Persuade** — the design is the product. Success is one scroll that answers "she is my person" and "this door is mine", ending in a WhatsApp message that was never demanded.
- **The three signatures, resolved by restraint.** DESIGN requires the mark, one classical painting, and Jung's voice inside ~1.5 mobile screens. Rather than adding an opening plate (which would contradict CONCEPT §7.1's type-led hero) or borrowing section 5's Jung passage into the hero, the hero stays disciplined: lockup · positioning sentence · lead · CTAs, with the portrait as a modest plate-like slot, and the credencial as a thin rule-bound strip. The first Instagram square then crests into view on its own around 1.3–1.5 screens, and her paired passage arrives on the first tap. **Nothing decorative is added; the constraint is met by cutting.**
- **Hero topology.** Desktop is an asymmetric spread — the reading column left at its natural 60–72ch, the portrait right as a 4/5 editorial plate with a marginalia caption in her voice ("quem recebe você"). The manuscript page with its illumination: the world's lockup and her portrait, side by side, each in its own voice. Stacks below `md`.
- **Focal moment.** One per page, as DESIGN demands: the wow slot (Cosmos on wide viewports, A Lâmina otherwise) at section 6, the scroll's midpoint. The un-crop is a second interactive moment but a quiet one — it lives inside a section rather than owning a screen, and it never overlays the page.
- **Tonal rhythm.** The scroll is parchment throughout with exactly two breaks: `parchment-deep` under the Brasil e exterior band, and the Cosmos's dark canvas. Terracotta stays the one recurring accent; the plates carry all saturation.
- **Anti-goals.** No tracked-caps eyebrow above every section (DESIGN names that as scaffolding). No card grid. No second wow competing with the slot. No plate behind text. No sticky anything.

## 1. Requirements & Constraints

- **REQ-001**: All eleven CONCEPT §6 sections ship, in the scroll order the map declares: Hero · Credencial · Instagram · Dois caminhos · O sintoma como chamado · Cosmos/A Lâmina · Sobre digest · Brasil e exterior · Como é começar · Vozes · Contato.
- **REQ-002**: Every visitor-facing string reads from the `page-inicio` global or from A Clínica — never hardcoded in a component (master plan GUD-002). Chrome-level strings (section scaffolding a visitor cannot edit meaningfully: aria labels, the "ver no Instagram" affordance, the un-crop close control) live in `messages/{pt,en}.json`.
- **REQ-003**: Both locales render. Untranslated fields fall back to Portuguese through Payload's `fallback: true` (RISK-001 of the master plan); nothing crashes or renders empty because English is missing.
- **REQ-004**: The Instagram bridge is the tile row decided above, and it ships **with the un-crop interaction working on labeled placeholder tiles** — the behavior is demonstrable before a single real image exists (REQ-010 of the master plan).
- **REQ-005**: The wow slot is exclusive: the Cosmos on wide viewports with motion allowed, **A Lâmina** otherwise. Never both mounted, never an empty hole (CONCEPT §7.4, DESIGN §5). A Lâmina's own reduced-motion form is the whole plate at rest with its captions listed — no travel.
- **REQ-006**: AEO front-load (master plan REQ-012): the first screen of content carries who · what · for whom · how to reach — the lockup, her positioning sentence verbatim, the credential strip, and a WhatsApp CTA — as semantic HTML with exactly one `h1`.
- **REQ-007**: Vozes is consent-gated structurally and **absent at zero records** (master plan SEC-002); `ReviewsJsonLd` is emitted only when at least one testimonial renders.
- **SEC-001**: The un-crop and A Lâmina read nothing about the visitor: no cookie, no storage, no measurement beyond the site-wide aggregate analytics. State is component-local.
- **CON-001**: Online-only. No section, alt text, caption, or placeholder label may imply a physical consulting room (master plan CON-001).
- **CON-002**: Layered architecture (master plan CON-003): `infrastructure/payload/` accessor → `domain/inicio/` type + mapper + action → route fetches and passes props → `view/inicio/` renders. No component sees a raw Payload shape; nothing in `src/domain/` imports React, Next, or next-intl.
- **CON-003**: Client components only for the interactive islands — the tile row, A Lâmina, and the slot that chooses between Cosmos and Lâmina. The nine prose sections are server components.
- **CON-004**: No sticky CTA, no floating bubble, no popup, no modal. The un-crop expands **inline within its section**; an inline expansion is not a popup, and nothing is allowed to overlay the page.
- **CON-005**: `pnpm build` peaks near 3.5 GB RSS and stays pressure-sensitive; when it dies, fall back to `tsc` + `next dev` + the Vercel preview (master plan CON-004).
- **GUD-001**: DESIGN.md governs every visual call: all-serif, plates carry the only saturation, terracotta the one recurring accent, flat on parchment, near-sharp edges, one drop cap per page opening, the two-voices rule on every text element.
- **GUD-002**: No tracked-caps eyebrow above every section — DESIGN §6 names that as scaffolding, not voice. The `.tracked` label is used where the world genuinely speaks (plate captions, the credential strip, the un-crop's gallery label).
- **PAT-001**: Placeholder policy (master plan REQ-005): every missing asset renders as `MediaPlaceholder` with a label saying what belongs there. The portrait, the Instagram tiles and A Lâmina's canvas are all placeholders at launch.
- **PAT-002**: Compose the Phase 5 primitives rather than re-implementing them: `MediaPlaceholder`, `Plate`, `JungPassage`, `CredentialLine`, `WhatsAppCta`, `AvailabilityLine`, `RichTextProse`.
- **PAT-003**: One concept per file, named exports, no barrels.

## 3. Alternatives

- **ALT-001**: Keep the configurable section-order model (`src/domain/sections/`) and let the CMS reorder the home. Rejected: master plan ALT-002 already settled that CONCEPT's pages are fixed compositions; a reorderable home lets an editor break a scroll whose whole argument is its sequence.
- **ALT-002**: Move `src/view/home/*.tsx` into `src/view/inicio/` rather than rewriting them. Rejected: they are typed against `Home`, and four of the five (Hero, About, Contact, Voices) change shape against the new eleven-section model. `Pillars.tsx` genuinely belongs to `/analise`, whose data is already seeded into `page-analise` — TASK-037 rebuilds it there rather than carrying an unmounted component across two phases.
- **ALT-003**: Render the un-crop in a modal/lightbox. Rejected: CONCEPT bans popups and overlays outright (CON-004 here); an inline expansion also keeps the row's context visible, which is what makes the crop→canvas reveal legible.
- **ALT-004**: Gate Cosmos vs A Lâmina with CSS (`hidden lg:block`). Rejected: `hidden` still mounts the component, so a phone would pay for a WebGL scene it never sees. One client boundary choosing between them is the only honest form of "never both mounted" (REQ-005).
- **ALT-005**: Anchor the un-crop animation to the crop's real rectangle inside the full canvas. Rejected for now: the CMS stores `crop` and `full` as two independent media with no rect relating them, so the true shared-element transform would need data she has no way to supply. The centered cross-fade + scale-out delivers the same reading ("the canvas grows around the square you know") with the data actually on hand.
- **ALT-006**: Keep `page-inicio.credencial.items` and render the strip from the page global. Rejected: the strip appears on every core page (CONCEPT §8.8), so a per-page copy would drift the moment she edits one of them.

## 7. Risks & Assumptions

- **RISK-001**: A Lâmina is the largest genuinely new interaction in this plan and its quality is bounded by a painted canvas that does not exist yet. Mitigation: the scroll mechanics ship and are verified against the placeholder frame, so the only change when her plate lands is a CMS upload — but the _aesthetic_ judgment is deferred to that moment and stated as such rather than claimed now.
- **RISK-002**: The un-crop's centered cross-fade is an approximation of the true shared-element reveal (ALT-005). If it reads as a plain fade rather than as "the canvas grew around my square", the fix is data (a stored crop rect), not more animation.
- **RISK-003**: Deleting `src/domain/home/Home.ts` removes the last code copy of her rewritten pillars intro, which Phase 1 rescued from the database. Mitigation: TASK-005 moves it into `src/payload/seed/pages.ts` **before** the delete, and it also remains in `docs/content-export-2026-08.md`; verify the seed writes it before removing the module.
- **RISK-004**: A client boundary around the wow slot could opt the route out of static rendering, as `loading.tsx` did in Phase 3. Mitigation: TEST-003 asserts `● (SSG)` explicitly rather than assuming it.
- **ASSUMPTION-001**: `page-inicio.credencial` holds no production data (it was seeded empty in Phase 4 and prod has not been edited), so dropping the tab loses nothing. Verify against the local row before generating the migration.
- **ASSUMPTION-002**: English copy falls back to Portuguese wherever she has not translated (master plan RISK-001) — accepted, not a defect of this page.
- **ASSUMPTION-003**: The Cosmos needs no changes; Phase 5's `StarField` seam and its existing reduced-motion poster are consumed as they are.
