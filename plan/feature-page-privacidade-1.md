# Introduction

![Status: Completed](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-Completed-brightgreen)

This plan executes **TASK-042** of [`plan/architecture-site-restructure-1.md`](./architecture-site-restructure-1.md) under its per-page ritual (PAT-001): the footer-only privacy page, built from the `page-privacidade` global that Phase 4 created and the primitives Phase 5 built, following the precedents in [`plan/feature-page-primeira-conversa-1.md`](./feature-page-primeira-conversa-1.md) and [`plan/feature-page-inicio-1.md`](./feature-page-inicio-1.md).

The page's job, in CONCEPT §6's words: **finished text, short and honest — what the site keeps (your language choice, in your browser; anonymous aggregate visit statistics) and what it never does (identify you, read you, personalise for you); the note you write composes a WhatsApp link in your own browser.**

It is the only page in the eight-page map whose entry in CONCEPT §6 is a promise about tone rather than a list of sections, and it is the only one that is **a launch gate**. The Phase 5 notes name it so: `@vercel/analytics` now ships in the frontend layout, and the site must not become indexable before this page names those aggregate statistics honestly (SEC-001). Until this pass, `/privacidade` carried the pre-CONCEPT pt-BR LGPD draft — hardcoded, banner-marked "Rascunho — em revisão", with a hand-rolled visual breadcrumb no other page has, and rendering **Portuguese prose at `/en/privacy`**. That last one was the site's worst untranslated surface: an anglophone opening a privacy statement to decide whether to trust the site, and being answered in a language they cannot read.

The page also makes the site's measurement position legible, and that position is unusual enough to be worth stating rather than burying: **there is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.** No analytics cookie, no form, no identifier, nothing personalised. A banner would also violate CONCEPT §12's own ban on popups — so the honest page and the design rule agree, and the page says so in its second sentence.

**One thing this page is not, and says nowhere that it is: legally reviewed.** Every sentence on it states something this repository decides, and I checked each one against the code before writing it (see §2's findings). The _shape_ of a data-processing notice under the LGPD is a lawyer's question, not a designer's, and §7 records the three gaps I chose not to invent.

## 0. Shape brief

The direction contract was settled before this page — PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md define the world, CONCEPT §6 fixes the sections and their order — so the shape work resolved only what was materially open. Mode: **Reassure**; success is a reader who believes the page and leaves, and the design's whole job is to not get in the way of that.

- **The page has no opening in the CMS beyond a tab, and the tab has a field it should not use.** The `abertura` tab (added for every page by TASK-036's precedent) carries `eyebrow` + `heading` + rich-text `body`. The `eyebrow` earns nothing here: the only text it could plausibly hold is the page's own name, which the footer link, the tab title and the breadcrumb already say, and DESIGN §6 names a tracked-caps kicker over a heading as scaffolding rather than voice. It was **removed from the schema** rather than left as a field that renders nowhere.
- **Sequence: answer, then be specific, then be complete.** Abertura (the whole page in three sentences) → **o que o site guarda** (three items, short) → **o que o site nunca faz** (five items, longer) → **sobre o bilhete** → **quem responde por isso**. The first four describe the _site_, which keeps almost nothing. The fifth describes the _conversation_, which is where data actually exists.
- **Focal moment: the asymmetry of the two lists, and nothing else.** No plate, no wow set-piece, no CTA, no client component of its own. The page's one designed pleasure is structural — a short list of what is kept, then a longer one of what is never done, so the reader sees the shape of the answer before reading a word of it. Every `nuncaFaz` title opens with a negation, which does the rest.
- **Tonal rhythm: exactly one event.** Parchment throughout with `parchment-deep` under **o que o site nunca faz** — the relief belongs to the longer list, and a second sheet is how the asymmetry becomes visible rather than merely true.
- **The `h1` is the page's name, and the answer is the first line of prose.** "Privacidade", at Headline scale, then the dropcapped lead opening on _"Este site quase não guarda nada."_ A cleverer `h1` was drafted and rejected (§3, ALT-002): on the one page that has to be believed, a heading that performs is a heading that costs.
- **The lead answers the whole page in three sentences**, because most people who open this page are checking one thing and leaving: what stays in your browser, what the visit statistics are, and why there is no cookie notice.
- **Anti-goals.** No "Rascunho — em revisão" banner (CONCEPT §6 asks for finished text). No legal-boilerplate padding — no headings for their own sake, no restated definitions, no "we may from time to time". No visual breadcrumb (no other page renders one). No WhatsApp CTA, no availability line, no credencial strip: this page's terminal is a mailto for exercising a right, not an ask. No scare quotes around "cookies". No smugness — the page states facts and never congratulates itself for them.

## 1. Requirements & Constraints

- **REQ-001**: All four CONCEPT §6 sections ship in the map's order — abertura · o que o site guarda · o que o site nunca faz · sobre o bilhete — plus a fifth the map has no room for (**quem responde por isso**), appended so the four keep their order and relative position.
- **REQ-002**: Every visitor-facing string reads from `page-privacidade` or from A Clínica — never hardcoded (master plan GUD-002). Only two `<dt>` labels ("Responsável", "Contato") live in `messages/{pt,en}.json`, which is the fee-row-label class of scaffolding the brief allows.
- **REQ-003**: **Both locales are written, not fallen back to.** Every field is `localized: true` and both `PRIVACIDADE_DEFAULTS` and the seed carry pt _and_ en. This page is the documented exception to the master plan's RISK-001: elsewhere a Portuguese fallback is an accepted rough edge; here it is the defect the task was gating on.
- **REQ-004**: AEO front-load (master plan REQ-012): the abertura carries what the site keeps · what it never does · why there is no consent banner, as semantic HTML with exactly one `h1`.
- **REQ-005**: Every claim on the page is **checkable in this repository**, not asserted. The cookie's name, lifetime, `SameSite` and payload come from `src/i18n/routing.ts`; the aggregate statistics are what `@vercel/analytics` collects, mounted in `(frontend)/[locale]/layout.tsx`; the absence of forms is a grep; the bilhete's mechanics are `whatsappUrlFromPhone` + `BilheteNote`; the third browser write is `cosmos:show` in `infrastructure/browser/cosmosPreference.ts`.
- **REQ-006**: The page must be true **whether or not** Web Analytics is switched on in the Vercel dashboard — that is an owner action on Luiza's account (CON-005), reported rather than done by TASK-054. Nothing on the page changes when it is flipped.
- **REQ-007**: The three facts the pre-CONCEPT draft got right survive the rewrite in some form: **who is responsible for the data plus how to reach them**, the **LGPD rights sentence**, and **sigilo profissional** under the Código de Ética Profissional do Psicólogo.
- **REQ-008**: CONCEPT §11's symbols rule gets one sentence: symbols index content, never the visitor — no birthdate, no "your sign", no personalised reading, anywhere, ever; the wheel on `/analise` is vocabulary.
- **SEC-001**: The page reads nothing about the visitor and adds no cookie, no storage, no measurement and no per-visitor branching of its own. It has no client component at all.
- **CON-001**: Online-only. No item, row, label or metadata may imply a room a patient walks into.
- **CON-002**: Layered architecture (master plan CON-003): `infrastructure/payload/` accessor → `domain/privacidade/` type + mapper + action → route fetches and passes props → `view/privacidade/` renders. Nothing in `src/domain/` imports React, Next or next-intl.
- **CON-003**: **PAT-002's plate is exempted on this page, and the exemption is recorded** (§2, decision 4). CONCEPT §7 and DESIGN §5 both say every page carries at least one plate; this is the argued exception, not an omission.
- **CON-004**: No visible draft banner, and no claim that legal review has happened. The page states only what it can support; the admin description tells her a legal read is still owed before launch.
- **GUD-001**: DESIGN.md governs every visual call — all-serif, terracotta the one recurring accent, flat on parchment, near-sharp edges, one `.dropcap` for the page, the two-voices rule on every text element.
- **GUD-002**: Operational facts in body type, never decorative small type (DESIGN's Marginalia-Is-Voice rule). The identification and the contact are the one address on the site somebody writes to in order to exercise a right, and a right nobody can find is not a right.
- **PAT-001**: Compose the Phase 5 primitives and the shared page grammar rather than re-implementing them: `PageSection`, `SectionHeading`, `SectionLink`, `RichTextProse`, `Ornament`.
- **PAT-002**: One concept per file, named exports, no barrels.

- **Eight decisions the task text left open.**
  1. **A fifth section, appended.** CONCEPT §6's four tabs have no home for who is responsible, the LGPD rights sentence, or sigilo profissional — and dropping all three with the old page would have made the new one true and incomplete at the same time, which on a privacy page is a way of being untrue. The added tab is **`responsavel` — "Quem responde por isso"**, and it earns its place by describing something the four tabs do not: the first four are about the _site_, which keeps almost nothing; this one is about the _conversation_, which is where data actually exists. It goes **after** `bilheteNota` rather than before, so the page reads as a sequence — the site keeps this, never does that, the note is composed in your browser, and once the message arrives here is who holds it, what the law guarantees you, and what covers the sessions themselves. CONCEPT's four keep their order and their relative position, which is the least invasive reading of "the map's order is law". Rejected: folding the three facts into `nuncaFaz` (they are not things the site never does), into the abertura (three sentences cannot carry a rights notice), or dropping them (see §3, ALT-001).

  2. **`eyebrow` was removed from the schema, not left unrendered.** The old page's eyebrow printed the page's own name. DESIGN §6 calls a kicker over a heading scaffolding, and the footer link, the `<title>` and the breadcrumb already answer "where am I". Keeping the field would have left her a box that types into nothing, which is a worse defect than a `DROP COLUMN` on a global Phase 4 seeded empty. Rejected: repurposing it as a "last updated" line — see §7, risk 4.

  3. **The defaults are keyed by locale, and only on this page.** Every other page carries one Portuguese `*_DEFAULTS` and lets Payload's `fallback: true` serve Portuguese to an English reader (master plan RISK-001, accepted). That trade is wrong here for a specific reason rather than a general one: this is the page an anglophone opens _to decide whether the site can be trusted with them_, and answering in a language they cannot read is the single worst place a fallback can land. So `PRIVACIDADE_DEFAULTS` is a `Record<Locale, Privacidade>`, `privacidadeFromPayload(doc, locale)` takes the locale, `getPrivacidade` catches into the right one, and `/en/privacy` degrades to English even with Payload switched off entirely. The cost is one extra argument on one mapper and a documented deviation; the alternative was shipping the exact defect this task was gating on.

  4. **No plate, and the PAT-002 exemption is recorded here.** CONCEPT §7 and DESIGN §5 both say every page carries at least one; this page carries none, on four grounds. (a) CONCEPT §6 gives it no content sections — it is the only entry in the map that describes a tone rather than a list, and DESIGN places plates "at section-scale moments", which a page of two scannable lists and three short paragraphs does not have. (b) The plate is the site's saturation and its one non-literal element; this page's entire rhetorical work is being literal about what the site does, and a painting between "what the site keeps" and "what it never does" would be the page contradicting its own sentence. (c) REQ-005's placeholder policy makes the cost concrete: with no painting chosen the slot renders a labeled frame, which is an admission of incompleteness printed in the one place a reader is deciding whether the site is trustworthy. (d) The page is footer-only and deliberately short; a full editorial moment would be the largest thing on it. **The exemption is scoped to this page and to this reasoning** — it is not a precedent for `/perguntas` or any other short page, both of which have sections plates can sit beside.

  5. **The identification is composed from A Clínica, not written into the page.** Her name, her role, the CRP and the email are cross-page facts held once (REQ-003), and the identification a reader would act on has to be the same string the colophon binds on every page — one CMS edit must move both. `clinica.credential` is blank until she confirms her CRP in writing (DEP-005), so the line joins only non-empty parts and gains ` · CRP …` the moment she fills it, rather than printing an empty separator or advertising a registration the site has not verified. The contact falls to the WhatsApp link if `clinica.email` is ever blanked, so the row can never be an empty cell.

  6. **`required: true` on both list rows, so a half-typed disclosure can never be saved.** The mapper drops a row missing either half — a `<dt>` with no `<dd>` is neither valid nor readable — but on this page a silently dropped row is a _disclosure that disappeared_, which is the worst failure mode the page has. Making both fields required in the admin closes the gap at the source instead of relying on the mapper's guard. Both `*_items` tables are empty in dev and prod, so the constraint costs nothing to add; the handoff says what to do if `migrate:create` fights, and says not to solve it by dropping `required`.

  7. **`clinica.privacyLine` is deliberately not rendered here.** It is a _cross-page_ one-liner by its own admin description ("A linha curta que resume, **em todo o site**, o que ele guarda e o que não faz"), and on the page that says the same thing at length it could only repeat the lead — which on the site's brand-defining claim is a copy defect, not a nicety. It is unset today and nothing in the repository renders it. The handoff carries the suggested pt/en text and recommends its home: the footer's _O mundo_ column under the `privacidade` link, where a reader is deciding whether the link is worth a click. `view/chrome/` is not this task's to touch.

  8. **No CTA, no availability line, no credencial strip.** CONCEPT §6 gives this page no "começar" section, the footer already carries the credential strip and the availability line, and a terracotta WhatsApp block on the privacy page would be the site asking for something on the one page where it is explaining what it does not take. The page's terminal is a `mailto:` for exercising a right and a quiet link to `/primeira-conversa`.

  - **No visible "Rascunho — em revisão" banner.** CONCEPT §6 asks for finished text, and a page that announces its own provisionality is a page nobody trusts. ## 3. Alternatives

- **ALT-001**: Ship only CONCEPT §6's four sections and let the responsibility statement, the LGPD rights sentence and sigilo profissional go with the old page. Rejected: the two lists describe the _site_, which keeps almost nothing, and stopping there implies that is the whole story. It is not — a WhatsApp conversation is data she holds, and a privacy page that omits who holds it, what the reader can ask for, and what covers the sessions is short at the cost of being true.
- **ALT-002**: An `h1` that performs the answer — "Este site quase não guarda nada." Rejected, though it was drafted and it front-loads harder: on the one page that has to be _believed_, a heading that reads as a flourish costs more than the plainness gains, and a reader arriving from a footer link labeled "Privacidade" should land on a page that says so. The sentence survives verbatim as the lead's first line, under the drop cap, which is where it does its work.
- **ALT-003**: Render `clinica.privacyLine` as a standfirst under the `h1`. Rejected: it is a cross-page one-liner summarising what the site keeps and never does, which is exactly what the lead below it says — so setting them one above the other repeats the site's brand-defining claim twice in one screen. See decision 7 for where it should live instead.
- **ALT-004**: Keep the `eyebrow` field and render the page's name in tracked caps, as the old page did. Rejected: DESIGN §6 names it scaffolding, and three surfaces already answer "where am I". Removing the field beat leaving it unrendered, because a CMS box that types into nothing is a trap.
- **ALT-005**: Ship a labeled `MediaPlaceholder` plate frame between the two lists, satisfying PAT-002 structurally the way `/primeira-conversa` did. Rejected: CONCEPT §6 asks for finished text, and a self-declared draft on a privacy page undermines the exact quality the page exists to produce. Rejected in favour of a `<dl>` with both fields `required` in the admin: a description list is what a named thing with a description is, it is what an assistant reads cleanly, and requiring both at the source is a better answer to the half-typed row than a forgiving renderer.

## 7. Risks & Assumptions

- **RISK-001**: **A lawyer has not read this page, and this is the one page on the site where a lawyer's read is worth more than a designer's.** Mitigation: nothing on the page claims legal review; every sentence states something checkable in the repository; the LGPD rights sentence and the sigilo profissional duty are both present and each is its own CMS field so either can be corrected without touching prose. Three gaps were left rather than invented, and they are the questions to put to a lawyer: **(a)** whether a stated legal basis / hipótese de tratamento is needed for the WhatsApp conversation; **(b)** whether a retention period must be stated for messages she has received; **(c)** whether the notice needs a version or "last updated" line, and in what form. On (c) my own read: a date rendered from a constant goes stale the first time she edits a word, and one rendered from the global's `updatedAt` moves on a typo fix — so if a lawyer wants a version line, the honest form is a field she sets by hand.
- **RISK-002**: **The page can silently become false.** It enumerates cookies and browser-storage keys, so adding an analytics tool, a form, an embed that reads the reader, or a second `localStorage` key breaks it without breaking a test. Mitigation: the `guarda` array's admin description says so in her own panel ("Se o site deixar de guardar alguma destas coisas, apague o item — esta lista só vale se estiver exata"), and TEST-006 makes it a check the TASK-051 sweep can carry. The third item in particular is tied to the Cosmos dismiss control surviving.
- **RISK-003**: **The English pass is the whole point and is unverified end to end.** If the en seed pass fails or is skipped, `/en/privacy` falls back to Portuguese — the exact defect this task gated on — and it would fail quietly, because Payload's fallback is designed to look like success. Mitigation: `PRIVACIDADE_DEFAULTS.en` makes English the code-level fallback too, so the only path back to Portuguese is a _stored_ Portuguese value under the en locale; and the handoff asks the integrator to read both locales back from the database after seeding.
- **RISK-004**: `migrate:create` may emit `SET NOT NULL` for the two lists' newly required `text` columns. Mitigation: both `*_items*` tables are empty in dev and in prod (Phase 4 seeded the five unbuilt globals with structure and no copy), so the statement succeeds; if it fails, prepend a `DELETE FROM` per the Phase 4 precedent rather than dropping `required`, which is load-bearing (decision 6).
- **RISK-005**: **The PAT-002 plate exemption could be read as a licence.** Mitigation: decision 4 scopes it to this page and to its four grounds, and states that it is not a precedent for `/perguntas` or any other short page. If the owner overrules it, the change is one `plate` group on the `bilheteNota` tab plus a `<Plate />` at the foot of that section — no restructuring.
- **ASSUMPTION-001**: `page-privacidade` holds no production data (Phase 4's own notes: the five unbuilt globals were seeded empty and prod has not been edited), so removing `eyebrow` and tightening two columns loses nothing.
- **ASSUMPTION-002**: The site is `noindex` until `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_INDEXABLE` flips, and this page is a condition of that flip rather than of the merge.
- **ASSUMPTION-003**: Vercel Web Analytics collects paths, referrers and countries and sets no cookies — as the vendor documents and as Phase 5 verified on `next start`. The page's wording holds whether or not the dashboard switch is on.
